Cancellation Policy

This Cancellation Policy outlines the terms and conditions regarding the cancellation of bookings made with Indtara.

1. Deposits

Deposits paid to secure a reservation are strictly non-refundable. This amount is used to secure supplier commitments and operational services on your behalf.

2. Cancellation Fees

Cancellation fees may apply depending on the timing of the cancellation and the commitments made to third-party suppliers (hotels, transportation, local guides, etc.).

Indtara reserves the right to retain amounts already committed to suppliers that are non-recoverable.

3. Supplier Terms

Specific cancellation terms from third-party suppliers (e.g., airlines, luxury hotels) will be passed on to the traveler and may be more restrictive than Indtara's general policy.

4. Force Majeure

In cases of force majeure (natural disasters, political instability, etc.), Indtara will work to provide equivalent alternatives or recover funds from suppliers, but cannot guarantee full refunds for non-recoverable costs.

5. Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend that all travelers obtain comprehensive travel insurance that includes cancellation coverage to protect against unforeseen circumstances.
