Cancellation Policy

This Cancellation Policy outlines the cancellation terms, refund eligibility, and fee structure for bookings curated by Indtara Experiences and operated by our licensed destination partners.

1. Cancellation Timeline and Fee Structure

In the event that you must cancel your experience, the following fees apply based on when the written cancellation request is received by Indtara Experiences and our operating partners:

  • More than 30 days before departure: Partial refund depending on non-recoverable costs (such as pre-booked flights, national park permits, hotels, and local logistics already committed to suppliers by the operator).
  • Between 30 and 15 days before departure: 50% cancellation fee.
  • Less than 15 days before departure: Non-refundable (100% cancellation fee).

2. Payments and Operator Responsibility

Because all travel payments are processed directly by the local operating partner (such as World Immigration Private Limited in India) responsible for executing the journey, any refund processing will be governed by the operator's capabilities and transaction conditions, facilitated by Indtara Experiences concierge support.

3. Force Majeure

No liability shall arise for cancellations, delays, or modifications resulting from events outside reasonable control, including but not limited to:

  • Natural disasters (earthquakes, severe storms, floods, etc.)
  • Pandemics or epidemics
  • Government restrictions, lockdowns, or visa suspension
  • Political instability, civil unrest, or war
  • Airline disruptions, cancellations, or schedule changes

4. Travel Insurance Recommendation

We strongly and encarecidamente advise all clients to purchase comprehensive travel insurance immediately after booking. This insurance should cover trip cancellation, travel delays, medical assistance, repatriation, accidents, and personal belongings. This serves as the primary protection against cancellation fees resulting from unexpected personal or medical emergencies.

5. Operational Changes by Partners

While unlikely, should our local operating partners need to modify or cancel a portion of your itinerary due to safety, weather, or operational constraints, Indtara Experiences will work diligently to arrange comparable alternative services. However, operational liability remains with the local operating partner.
