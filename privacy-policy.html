Privacy Policy

At Indtara Experiences, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

1. Data Collection

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express interest in obtaining information about our curated travel services, contact us through our website forms, or request custom itineraries. This information may include your name, email address, phone number, and preferences.

In accordance with GDPR, our forms include mandatory data consent checkboxes that you must explicitly select before submitting inquiries.

2. Use of Your Data

We process your personal information for purposes based on legitimate business interests, the fulfillment of our intermediary contract with you, compliance with our legal obligations, and/or your explicit consent. This includes coordinating your preferences with our licensed operating partners, such as World Immigration Private Limited in India, who execute the experiences.

3. Marketing Communications

If you explicitly consent (for example, by checking our newsletter and data consent checkboxes), Indtara Experiences may send you promotional emails, travel inspiration, updates, offers, and marketing communications related to our services.

You may opt-out or unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting info@indtaraexperiences.com.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a European or international visitor, you have several rights under the GDPR, including:

  • Access: The right to request copies of your personal data.
  • Rectification: The right to request correction of any inaccurate information.
  • Erasure: The right to request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Portability: The right to request transfer of your data to another organization.
  • Objection: The right to object to or restrict the processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@indtaraexperiences.com.

5. Storage and Security

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with operational, legal, and tax requirements. We secure this information using industry-standard measures, including SSL certificates and secure database operations.

6. Jurisdiction and Applicable Law

This Privacy Policy and any disputes arising from data processing are governed by Spanish and European legislation.
